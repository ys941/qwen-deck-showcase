## 🎯 What it is

**QwenDeck** is a personal AI deck you run yourself. It gives you your own model and a set of assistants that can actually *do* things: open apps, work in your real browser, search the web, read your spreadsheets, prepare you for an interview, and join a call to listen, speak and write the notes.

Everything is yours — the chats, the chatbots, the documents, the keys. You decide what the AI may touch, and anything risky waits for your approval.

## 💬 Chat & agents

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🧠 Your own model
Chat with a private model you control, with a **thinking** toggle for harder questions. Long conversations are summarised as they grow, so they never run out of room.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🔀 Pick any engine, per message
Switch the AI engine and model from a dropdown on any single message. If your main engine is asleep, an automatic fallback steps in — or switch fallback off and get an honest "not available" instead.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🤖 Agent mode
One toggle turns a chat into an agent that **plans, acts, checks its own work and recovers** from mistakes — then reports what it did. Multi-step tasks, not one-shot answers.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🖥️ Drives your desktop
Opens apps, clicks, types, reads windows and manages files on your computer — step by step, with destructive actions always gated behind your approval.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🌐 Works in your real browser
A companion browser extension lets the agent use **the browser you already use**, signed-in sites and all. Off by default, allowed site by site, with a block list that always wins and a log of every action.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🔎 Web search & deep research
Searches and reads the web on its own. Research mode digs deeper with more rounds and turns the findings into a **saved, shareable page** — ask for a report and get a link.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🏗️ Builds whole projects
Asks for a site or a tool? It writes every file in one go and shows you the real folder it created — no claiming files it never made.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🧩 Many chatbots, one deck
Create as many chatbots as you like, each with its own persona, engine, documents, keys and training. Switch between them from a side rail.

</td>
</tr>

## 🎧 Meeting assistant — meet **Vyom**

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🗓️ Knows your schedule
Reads your calendar and your inbox: **CC Vyom on an invite** and it schedules the meeting, reads the email thread for context and is ready at the start time. Only trusted senders can schedule it.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 📋 A brief before you walk in
Who's attending, what was decided last time, what's still open — prepared before the call, with a heads-up on your phone and a one-tap Skip.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🗣️ Listens, speaks, keeps up
Announces itself, knows who said what, reads the chat and shared slides, replies when addressed with a natural voice, and stops talking the moment someone talks over it.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🙋 Asks before it decides
When a question needs you, Vyom pings your phone and waits. It never commits you to anything on its own.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🧾 Notes worth reading
After the call: a designed notes page with **decisions, action items, key numbers and who spoke** — not a transcript dump. Export to PDF, Word or Markdown, or email it to attendees with one click (only when you press Send).

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### ✅ Follow-through
Action items become a task board with due dates and reminders, meetings go back on your calendar, and "**Catch me up**" summarises anything you missed. Vyom remembers across meetings.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 👔 Hiring mode
Interviewing someone? Load the job description and get a scorecard, a fit-for-the-role analysis, side-by-side candidate comparisons — and a private alert if answers sound AI-written.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🎭 Your choice of role
**Private mode** listens on your laptop and whispers suggestions to your phone. **Participant mode** joins the call and takes part. Switchable personas and languages.

</td>
</tr>
